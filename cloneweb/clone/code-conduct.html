1. Preamble

Pathé is a French family-owned group, present in the cinema industry for more than 125 years. Our reputation and the trust of our audiences, partners and employees depend on the integrity of every person who works under the Pathé name — and on the rigour with which we apply our ethical principles.

This Code of Conduct sets out the principles that guide the actions of every employee, executive, director and partner acting on behalf of the Pathé group, in every country where we operate. It is supplemented by more detailed policies on specific topics.

2. Our principles

Five principles summarise the ethical expectations of the Pathé group. They apply to every decision, every day.

  • Integrity — We act honestly, in compliance with applicable laws and the highest professional standards.
  • Respect — We treat every person with dignity, regardless of their role, background or beliefs.
  • Responsibility — We take responsibility for our decisions and their impact on people, communities and the planet.
  • Transparency — We communicate clearly and honestly, internally and externally.
  • Excellence — We pursue the highest quality in everything we do — and never at the expense of our principles.

3. Expected behaviour

Every employee and partner of Pathé is expected to behave with professionalism, courtesy and respect. This includes — but is not limited to — interactions with colleagues, audiences, suppliers, artists, journalists, regulators and public authorities.

Discriminatory, harassing or violent behaviour of any kind is strictly prohibited, both inside Pathé premises and in any context where a person may be perceived as acting on behalf of the group.

4. Conflicts of interest

A conflict of interest arises when personal interests, financial or otherwise, may influence — or appear to influence — the professional judgement of a Pathé employee. Employees must disclose any actual, potential or apparent conflict of interest to their manager and to the compliance team, and recuse themselves from any related decision.

5. Gifts & hospitality

Gifts and hospitality may be offered or accepted only when they are modest, occasional, and clearly linked to a legitimate business purpose. Cash and cash-equivalent gifts are prohibited. Specific thresholds and rules are described in the Gifts & Hospitality Policy.

6. Harassment & discrimination

Pathé is committed to providing a workplace free from harassment, discrimination and retaliation. This includes, but is not limited to, conduct related to gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, ethnicity, religion, political opinion or any other characteristic protected by law.

Any employee who witnesses or experiences conduct contrary to this principle is encouraged to report it — formally or informally — to their manager, the HR team or via the Pathé ethics line (see section 9).

7. Data protection

Pathé processes personal data of its employees, customers, audiences and partners in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable local laws. Every employee handling personal data is expected to follow the Pathé Data Protection Policy and the related procedures.

8. Environmental responsibility

Pathé is committed to reducing the environmental impact of its operations and to supporting the transition of the cinema industry. Employees are expected to apply the group's environmental policy, to report environmental risks and to suggest improvements whenever possible.

9. Whistle-blowing

Pathé operates a confidential whistle-blowing line — managed by an independent third party — accessible to all employees and external stakeholders. Reports may be made anonymously. Pathé strictly prohibits any retaliation against a person who reports an alleged breach in good faith.

10. Sanctions

Any breach of this Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary measures, up to and including dismissal, and — where applicable — civil or criminal proceedings. Sanctions are decided on a case-by-case basis, taking into account the seriousness of the breach and its consequences.

The full text of this Code is available as a PDF on the Pathé intranet. For external requests, please contact our compliance team.
