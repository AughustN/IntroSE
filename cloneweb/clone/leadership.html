The committee

Six people,
one direction.

Jérôme Seydoux

Chairman

Fourth-generation cinema family. Chair of Pathé since 1999 and President of the Fondation Jérôme Seydoux-Pathé. A passionate advocate for cinema as a cultural good.

Arnaud Bossé

Chief Executive Officer

Joined Pathé in 2010. Previously head of distribution at StudioCanal. Leads the group's global strategy across production, distribution and exhibition.

Sophie Dulac

Head of Production

Producer of more than 40 feature films. Member of the Académie des Arts et Techniques du Cinéma. Oversees Pathé's production labels and international co-productions.

Marc Lacan

Head of Distribution

Twenty-five years in international film sales. Leads Pathé International and the group's distribution activities in France, the UK, Switzerland and the Netherlands.

Aïcha Konaté

Head of Exhibition

Former CEO of a major African cinema chain. Joined Pathé in 2018 to lead the cinema network across 8 countries and Pathé's expansion in West Africa.

Camille Ravier

Chief Financial Officer

Joined Pathé in 2015 from a major media group. Oversees finance, IT, legal, HR and the group's transformation and sustainability programmes.

Board of directors

A board that
mixes heritage
and renewal.

The Board of Directors of Pathé includes representatives of the Seydoux family, the group's senior executives and four independent members bringing expertise from media, finance, climate science and the cultural sector.

The Board meets four times a year and is supported by three committees — Audit & Risk, Nominations & Remuneration, and Sustainability & Impact.

The full governance documents — articles of association, board rules, committee charters — are available on request from the company's general secretariat.
