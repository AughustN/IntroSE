1. Data controller

The data controller for the processing described in this policy is Pathé SA, a French société anonyme registered under number 542 088 542 RCS Paris, with its registered office at 2 rue Lamennais, 75008 Paris, France.

For specific services (e.g. Pathé VOD, Pathé Thuis, Foundation website), the local subsidiary may act as a joint or independent data controller — in which case additional information is provided in the privacy notice of the relevant service.

2. Data we collect

Depending on how you interact with our services, we may collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Identification data — name, email address, postal address, phone number, date of birth (for age verification).
  • Account data — login credentials, preferences, loyalty programme membership.
  • Transaction data — purchases, bookings, payment method (the latter is processed by our payment provider and is not stored by us).
  • Browsing data — IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, time spent, referring URL.
  • Content data — reviews, ratings, comments, photos you submit.
  • Location data — approximate location based on IP, or precise location if you grant explicit consent.

3. How we use your data

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To provide the services you have requested (ticketing, VOD, account management).
  • To process payments and prevent fraud.
  • To communicate with you about your account, your orders and our services.
  • To send you marketing communications, where you have given your consent (or where permitted by law).
  • To improve our services, perform analytics and personalise your experience.
  • To comply with our legal and accounting obligations.

4. Legal basis

We rely on the following legal bases under the GDPR:

  • Contract — to provide the services you have signed up for.
  • Consent — for marketing communications, non-essential cookies and any optional feature requiring your agreement.
  • Legitimate interest — to improve our services, ensure security, prevent fraud and manage our business, in a way that does not override your fundamental rights.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with applicable laws, in particular accounting and anti-money-laundering obligations.

5. Sharing & transfers

We do not sell your personal data. We share your data only with the following categories of recipients, on a need-to-know basis:

  • Pathé group entities, for the purposes described above.
  • Service providers acting as data processors on our behalf (IT, payment, customer service, marketing, analytics).
  • Public authorities, where required by law.
  • Business partners, only where you have given your explicit consent (e.g. a co-promotion).

When we transfer your data outside the European Economic Area, we do so only to countries with an adequacy decision from the European Commission, or under appropriate safeguards (standard contractual clauses, binding corporate rules).

6. Retention

We keep your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including to satisfy any legal, accounting or reporting requirements. Specific retention periods are set out in the privacy notice of each service.

7. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies on our websites. Some are strictly necessary for the website to function; others — analytics, personalisation, advertising — require your consent. You can manage your preferences at any time via the cookie settings accessible from the footer of every page.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right of rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right of erasure — ask us to delete your data, subject to legal obligations.
  • Right to restrict processing — ask us to limit how we process your data.
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interest, including profiling.
  • Right to withdraw consent — at any time, where processing is based on consent.
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

To exercise these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer using the details below.
