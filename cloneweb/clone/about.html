Milestones

A century
and a quarter.

1896

The Pathé brothers begin

Charles, Émile, Théophile and Jacques Pathé open a workshop selling phonographs in Paris — and quickly turn their attention to the new art of cinema.

1907

World's largest film producer

Pathé Frères becomes the largest film company in the world, producing more than 80% of the films shown in France and exporting to every continent.

1936

First Paris cinema circuit

Pathé opens its first dedicated cinema in Paris, beginning nearly nine decades of exhibition that continues today.

1990

Gaumont-Pathé alliance

Pathé and Gaumont form a joint exhibition venture, modernising the cinema experience across France.

2014

Fondation Jérôme Seydoux-Pathé

Jérôme Seydoux-Pathé opens the Foundation dedicated to the heritage of cinema, in a Renzo Piano-designed building in Paris.

2024

Expansion in West Africa

New Pathé theatres open in Ivory Coast, marking the company's accelerated expansion across francophone Africa.
