Sustainability.

A cinema that lasts is a cinema that listens. From the energy that powers our screens to the stories we choose to tell, every choice matters.

Three pillars

What we
work on, every day.

01 — Climate

Reduce, replace, restore

Cut our direct emissions 50% by 2030, switch our cinemas to 100% renewable electricity, and offset the rest with verified projects.

02 — Inclusion

A cinema for everyone

Accessible screenings, gender parity in our productions, support for emerging talent from under-represented backgrounds.

03 — Circularity

Less waste, more cinema

Eliminate single-use plastics from our food & beverage operations, redesign packaging, donate surplus food to local associations.
