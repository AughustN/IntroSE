About the Foundation

Where cinema's
memory lives.

Created in 2006 at the initiative of Jérôme Seydoux, the Foundation preserves and shares the heritage of Pathé — one of the most significant cinematic collections in the world.

Housed in a Renzo Piano-designed building nestled behind a Haussmannian facade in the 13th arrondissement of Paris, the Foundation invites the public to discover the inventions, personalities and stories that built cinema as we know it.

More than 80,000 objects, 30,000 photographs, 1,500 films and an extensive library — open to researchers, students and the simply curious.
