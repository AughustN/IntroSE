Our process

What to expect.

Step 01

Application

Send your CV and a short note telling us why you'd like to work with us. We read every application.

Step 02

First conversation

A 30-minute video call with the hiring manager — to know each other, discuss the role, answer your questions.

Step 03

Case study or visit

Depending on the role, a short take-home exercise or an in-person visit to your future team and workplace.

Step 04

Welcome aboard

An offer within 5 business days, a structured onboarding programme, and a mentor for your first 6 months.
