1. Purpose

Cinema is a mirror of the societies that produce it. Pathé believes that a more inclusive industry creates better stories — and serves audiences better. This statement sets out the concrete commitments of the Pathé group regarding diversity, equity and inclusion (DEI), both as an employer and as a producer and distributor of films.

2. Equal opportunity

Pathé guarantees equal opportunity in every stage of the employment lifecycle — recruitment, training, promotion, compensation and career transitions. Hiring and promotion decisions are based exclusively on skills, experience, performance and potential.

Pathé prohibits any form of discrimination related to gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, ethnicity, nationality, religion, political opinion, family situation or any other characteristic protected by law.

3. Fair pay

Pathé is committed to paying its employees fairly and competitively for the work they perform. We conduct regular gender pay audits in every country where we operate and publish the results in our annual Sustainability Report. Where unjustified pay gaps are identified, we take corrective action.

4. Diversity on screen

Pathé is committed to improving the representation of under-represented groups on screen and behind the camera. Our commitments include:

  • Reaching gender parity in the casting of lead roles in Pathé-produced films by 2028.
  • Increasing the share of under-represented voices in key creative positions (writers, directors, producers) to at least 40% by 2028.
  • Investing 5% of our annual production budget in films led by creators from under-represented backgrounds.
  • Publishing detailed diversity data on our productions every year, in line with industry reporting standards.

5. Suppliers & partners

Pathé expects its suppliers, contractors and partners to share its commitment to equal opportunity and to comply with applicable anti-discrimination laws. Supplier contracts include a specific clause on diversity and inclusion, and breaches may lead to the termination of the commercial relationship.

6. Training & awareness

Pathé runs regular training programmes for its employees on diversity, unconscious bias, inclusive leadership and respectful behaviour in the workplace. All managers attend a dedicated programme as part of their onboarding and at least every two years thereafter.

7. Reporting & accountability

Pathé publishes a detailed Diversity & Inclusion report every year, including workforce demographics, pay gap analysis, progress on the on-screen commitments and the actions taken as a result of the previous year's data. The report is reviewed by the Board's Sustainability & Impact Committee.

The DEI programme is overseen by the Chief People Officer, who reports directly to the CEO.

For any question on this statement, contact our DEI team.
